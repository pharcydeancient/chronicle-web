University of Calabar expels student after alleged phone theft

A Public Health student has been removed from the register following a campus disciplinary ruling.

By Maxwell · Calabar · 29 August 2026

The University of Calabar has expelled a student in the Department of Public Health after a disciplinary panel found him liable in the alleged theft of a mobile phone belonging to another student.

A brief from the registry said the decision followed the university’s student code. The student’s name was withheld in the public note.

Campus security has been visible at the main gate since the ruling.
